Small and slow AI

Where do small, local and slow AI projects sit in relation to large-scale systems? Drag the examples, stretch their pace ranges, add your own, and see how the picture changes. Your changes stay in your browser.

Select a point to edit it. Arrow keys move a focused point (hold Shift for larger steps).

How to read the chart

Dot
Where most of a project's time and effort sits.
Dashed bar
The spread from its slowest stage (top end) to its fastest stage (bottom end), for example slow, careful data collection behind a fast practical tool, or the reverse. It is not an error margin.
Scale
The size of the data and computation behind a system, counting any pretrained models it builds on.
Pace
How quickly a project is made and used. Slowness here means deliberation and attention, not only duration.

The original placements are interpretive judgements based on public descriptions of the projects, and they are open to disagreement. Moving a dot is a way of making that disagreement visible.
